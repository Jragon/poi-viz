# Archer Fountains (mostly in opposites)

This page is to look in depth at Archer Fountains, which is actually the reason I started making this version of the visualiser. Particularly archer fountains in split time opposites.

## Related literature (poi vids lol)

- [Dave Statik's body tracing series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IQ9yTEaWooc&list=PL63C80789D7D8681F&index=3). This is where I first saw the concept of the archer weave in opposites, ie tracing above and below the armpit (a cosmo maybe in Mel's new language?) I recommend the whole body tracing series, it's quite foundational in general to archers in same direction and opposites.
- [Fess' Archers Time](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-sHhmWe1Ml0). A fantastic collection of archer based body tracing sequences. All way too complex for me so far :')
- [Darrin's Anti Spin vs Archer series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E8ZNbwT_34o&list=PLX1qD0Stb-rsyZ4q16LT3lr7Z97ijjith). One of the other intros into archer weaving in opposites. Skills here help with the archer fountains and they compliment eachother very nicely.
